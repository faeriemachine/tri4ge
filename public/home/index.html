Act Now

Various links to contribute to various liberation causes around the world

Palestine

  • Vetted Gaza Evacuation Fundraiser List - Masterlist of fundraisers for Palestinian families in need, vetted by actual Palestinians
  • Operation Olive Branch - Masterlist of fundraisers for Palestinian families in need
  • Gaza Evacuation Relief Fund - Fundraisers vetted by Ottawa4Palestine
  • The Butterfly Project - More vetted fundraisers for Palestinians in need
  • Bees and Watermelons - Even more vetted fundraisers for Palestinians in need
  • Crips for eSims for Gaza - Easily donate money that goes towards buying eSims for Gazans
  • PCRF - Donate directly to PCRF, this is their Ramadan Campaign
  • CareForGaza - The Twitter page for the Care For Gaza non-profit charity that aims to help needy families in Palestine
  • Pious Projects Palestine - A number of fundraiser initiatives you can donate to, providing needy families with everything between food and water, to medical supplies and specialized healthcare.
  • UNRWA - The official website for the UNRWA
  • BDS Movement - The official website for the Boycott, Divest, Sanction movement
  • Decolonize Palestine - Some starter material for decolonizing your beliefs on Palestine

Sudan

  • UNICEF Sudan - Official website for UNICEF's Sudan relief fund
  • WFP Sudan - Offical website for World Food Programme's Sudan relief fund, providing emergency food aid to families in need
  • The Khartoum Kitchen Appeal - GoFundMe campaign to help feed the people in the Kharotum State area.
  • SudanFunds - Direct links to verified fundraising campaigns supporting Sudan.
  • One Million Sustainable Pads Campaign - GoFundMe campaign to distribute reusable pads to women and girls in IDP camps.
  • Sudan Emergency Medical Appeal - LaunchGood campaign to help the only pediatric hospital in Sudan to provide free healthcare to war victims, deliver babies, and treat children.
  • Urgent Appeal to Support LGBTIQ Community in Sudan - GoFundMe campaign to help support LGBT families in Sudan.

The DR Congo

  • Special Report: Conflict Minerals in The DR Congo - GenocideWatch article detailing how the genocide happening in the DRC is intertwined with metal and mineral mining for electronics and aerospace companies.
  • Friends Of The Congo Goma Fundraiser - GoFundMe campaign to help the residents in the city of Goma.

Yemen

  • Yemen Emergency Fund - Human Appeal USA's fundraiser for the children and families living in Yemen.

Haiti

  • UNICEF Haiti - Official website for UNICEF's page for the Haiti crisis.

Turtle Island

  • Native Land - An interactive digital map that shows you who's land you're living on.
  • Mother nation - A Native-led nonprofit offering healing services, advocacy, mentorship, and homeless prevention for Native and Indigenous women and girls in the Washington State.
  • Indian Residental School Survivors Society - A Canadian charity commited to supporting survivors of the Indian Residential School system, including counselling, advocacy, and community outreach.
  • North American Traditional Indigenous Food Systems - An organization that works towards Indigenous food revival and educational support.
  • American Indian Community Development Corporation - An organization that provides housing and entrepreneurial programs to homeless Indigenous individuals suffering from substance use disorders.
  • MIGIZI - A Minneapolis oragnization that seeks to provide support for Indigenous youth that nurtuers their educational, social, economic and cultural development.
  • Native Land Conservancy - A Native-led land conservation that aims to protect, share and restore land and water for both people and animals.
