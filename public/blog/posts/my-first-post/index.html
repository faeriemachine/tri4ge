Baby's First Blog Post

Thursday, February 13, 2025 | 979 words

| 2025 | Webdev

Hello everyone, and welcome to Tri4ge dot ICU, running on it's 2.0 layout!

This is an update that's been a long time coming, as the 1.0 layout of this site was amateur work and desperately in need of recoding from the ground up. Ever since I started working on 1.0's layout, I learned a lot more about coding and website structural integrity, and have been wanting to change the way this site looks and operates since I first published it in September of 2025. By that point, I had 1.0's layout in a semi-finished state for nearly a year, and was stuck with a dilemma between not publishing what I worked on over a year ago due to not being happy with my work, or publishing it anyway for the sake of getting my commission information and terms of service up (which I ended up not doing many commissions due to health problems). Of course, I ended up choosing the latter, opting to keep it up for the meantime since I had ran out of energy and motivation for coding after working on my last project (a personal website, which I won't link here, but it isn't impossible to find if you know where to look for it). Even so, I always had plans to code everything from scratch all over again, and I'm very glad to have found the opportunity to do just that.

Going in-depth about the creation of this website...

  • The site is made using 11ty as my static site generator. This was my second time having used it, and I adore it all the time. It makes everything look so clean, and I love all the features I was able to include on this site. I do have plans of checking out other SSG's like Astro and Hugo at some point, but for now, I think 11ty does what I need my website to do at this time.
  • The site uses flexbox as my choice of layout module, since it's my current favourite. It makes coding responsive layouts a breeze in my opinion, and it does what I need it to do perfectly!
  • The navigation is now in a sidebar codepiece rather than a header on desktop, and I think it looks way cleaner this way! Plus, it's perfectly responsive for mobile as well—a win-win in my opinion.
  • Speaking of responsive sites, I've worked hard to make this site even more accessible than it was! The light/dark mode toggle has remained from the site's last layout, but I've also added hover-to-play gifs over the 'play/pause gif' toggle buttons I used to have, as well as a 'back to top' button! Of course, there's a double-edged sword here where a lot of these features run using Javascript, which can be a hindrance to accessibility, but these things wouldn't work the same (or even at all) without it. I still try to keep my Javascript usage to a minimum as much as possible, but it's rather difficult to do this.
  • The /home page is much tidier, in my opinion. I've cut down on a lot of the info that was on it for organisation and accessibility reasons, and I think it looks a lot more clean this way. As much as I loved having my site stats and Last.FM scrobbler on there, I felt these just added unnecessary filler to what is supposed to be an art-focused website.
  • The /fursuits page was not included in this revamp and has since been taken down, but fear not—I do plan on doing something with this! My plan/dream is to code my own fursuit company website from scratch for when I start taking commissions for suits and selling my own premades! Keep an eye out for this project in the future!

Oh yeah, and this blog is a new addition to the site! I've always wanted a blog on this site, but at the time I didn't have the expertise or layout to really support a functioning blog. I almost went with something like BearBlog to host my blog posts, but I really wanted to host my own fully functioning blog. Luckily, 11ty is the perfect tool for this, and I'm able to have a ton of features with my blog, including tags, post dates, word counts, and a blog roll of my most recent posts on my home page! It's great, and I really look forward to utilising this blog more.

As for what I want to post on here in the future... I'm honestly not sure? I do have a blog on my personal website, so I don't want to repeat a lot of the content I have there onto here. But I think it's safe to say that I'll be including writings that pertain to furry culture, creative works, and perhaps the odd political piece here and there on this site.

All this being said, I'm always planning new and improved features to this site, so it's a project that I don't imagine will ever be 100% 'complete!' I have plans on doing 'wiki pages' for each of my fursonas that will be hosted on here, as well as getting a guestbook up and running and uploading some of my artwork pre-2023 (which admittedly isn't a lot, I went through a dry period during 2021 to 2022 where I created little to no artwork.)
