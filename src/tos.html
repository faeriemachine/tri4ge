B. Payment & fees

1. Invoices will be sent through Paypal and in USD only. If you'd like to tip me, you can do so when paying the invoice, or by visiting my Ko-fi.
2. I take payment upfront for anything $150 and under after accepting your commission request.
3. For pieces over $150, you have the option of paying part of the cost up-front and paying the rest once the sketch is completed. I will not continue working on the piece after the sketch is completed until the full payment is received.
4. If no payment is received within 72 hours, the commission will be canceled and your slot will be given to someone else.
5. Small edits and changes can be made at any time during the commission process, but larger adjustments will cost an additional fee of up to 30%, such as big changes to a clean sketch, or more than one total re-sketch of the commission's first draft. For both of our sake, please request changes as early into the commission process as possible.
6. I also take payment in the form of physical items that I'm looking for, equivalent to the price of the commission. Please take a look at my In Search Of list to see what I'm looking for.

C. Commission Process

1. Only clear, visual references of the character(s) or subject(s) are permitted for commissions outside of character design work. Any reference materials made using generative AI will not be accepted. I also work best with visual references for posing and scenery.
2. Turnaround time can take anywhere from 2 weeks to 3 months, depending on the commission type and my current health status. In the event of a major delay in turnaround time, all clients will be notified and potential refunds discussed (see criterion D.)
3. Due to the unpredictable nature of my disabilities, I cannot work with deadlines at this time.
4. Work in progress shots will be sent throughout the process of the commission for approval and edit requests. You also have permission to draw over my WIPs to show which specific edits you'd like, so long as you understand that the artwork will be in my style.
5. I can communicate through Telegram or Email, and you may contact me at any time through these methods on the status of your commission or place in the queue. You may also view updates on my Trello.
